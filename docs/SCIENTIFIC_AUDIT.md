# Scientific content audit

Reviewed 2026-09-12. Selected examples, not a systematic or exhaustive literature review. Metadata and abstracts checked through Europe PMC; linked PMIDs identify primary articles. Full-text methods were not independently replicated.

The 2023 review supports the twelve process labels and brief definitions. Each experimental claim is separately linked to its primary study. Limitations state the boundary of what the selected source establishes, not the absence of all evidence elsewhere.
